Biography

Luca Mundaca is a Brazilian singer, songwriter and guitarist known for her elegant voice and expressive guitar work. Singing in Portuguese, she creates a refined fusion of Bossa Nova, contemporary lounge, Brazilian jazz and MPB, shaping a sound that is both timeless and modern. Her music draws deeply from the rich traditions of Brazilian rhythm and harmony, blending classic Bossa Nova sensibilities with global influences to define her signature style.

Her acclaimed albums Day by Day and Primeiro highlight her velvety vocals, lyrical depth and intricate guitar arrangements, each offering a sophisticated blend of Bossa Nova traditions and jazz-infused improvisation. Luca's compositions evoke the gentle sway of Rio's seaside breeze, the warm acoustic textures reminiscent of João Gilberto, and the contemporary lounge aesthetic that invites relaxed, intimate listening.

Luca's artistry has earned international recognition: she is a winner of the Independent Music Award (World Fusion category), placed third in the International Songwriting Competition (World Music category), and has been featured on global Putumayo World Music compilations including Brazilian Lounge and Women of the World: Acoustic. Her music has been included in film and television, spanning Hollywood releases such as End of Watch to Brazilian telenovelas.

In 2024 she performed at Lincoln Center's Summer for the City in New York. Her song Há Dias has gathered millions of streams, and her catalogue reaches over 230,000 monthly listeners on Spotify.
